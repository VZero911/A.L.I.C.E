# Legal — licensing & IP (draft for counsel)

Our work is licensed. This file explains the intent; the operative text is the root [`LICENSE`](../LICENSE). None
of this is legal advice, and all of it is a **draft a lawyer must review** before any real use or value.

## Posture
- **Proprietary, private by default** (NORME #5). The Work is owned by V / the project entity; no use without
  written permission. This protects the IP while it is unaudited and pre-legal-frame.
- **Protective, not open-source.** We deliberately do not use a permissive licence (MIT/Apache) yet — once there
  is a legal entity and an audit, V can choose to open specific parts.
- **Patent stance.** Where an invention is novel (e.g. the signed-gasless-action flow, the index/allocation
  design), V may file before any public disclosure. Keep inventions private until counsel advises; mark such
  areas and do not publish them.

## Contributors
- Contributions are assigned to the project entity under a contributor agreement, in exchange for **POC**
  (soulbound, earned) and, where applicable, a share per [`allocation.json`](allocation.json).
- A definitive contributor agreement (CLA) is a to-do for counsel; until then contributions are accepted in good
  faith on the LICENSE terms and a not-yet-merged contribution can be withdrawn by either side.

## Value & compliance (hard gate)
- **No real value** (tokens, payments, transfers) until: the contracts are **audited**, there is a **legal
  entity** and a **regulatory frame**, and **V approves** (NORME #4, #7). POC is not money.
- Terms of service and a privacy policy are required before a public beta that touches users (beta runbook).
